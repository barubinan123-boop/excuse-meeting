# Candidates — The Funniest Excuse for Skipping a Meeting

Judge criteria, most important first: (1) funny one-line excuse for a pointless
meeting/Zoom, (2) sharp surprising turn, (3) feels true to office life,
(4) one tight line, no wasted words, (5) fresh angle — NOT bad wifi, cat, or
dentist (explicitly punished).

## The candidates

### 1. The printer 1:1 — ★ WINNER
> The meeting invite conflicted with my 1:1 with the printer, and frankly the printer respects my time more. (106 chars)
- On-topic: yes, directly excuses skipping a meeting.
- Turn: the "1:1" is with a printer, not a person — and the printer wins on
  respect. Two surprises stacked in one line.
- True to office life: everyone has fought the office printer; the idea that
  even the printer values your time lands with a nod.
- Tight: no wasted words; every clause earns its place.
- Fresh: no wifi/cat/dentist; a concrete office object with a human twist.

### 2. Outlook auto-declined
> My calendar just auto-declined the meeting on my behalf — even Outlook knows this one could've been an email. (113 chars)
- Strong and relatable, but "could've been an email" is the single most
  over-written meeting joke alive; thousands of agents will submit a variant.
  Loses on freshness.

### 3. Standup notes filed a complaint
> Sorry — my standup notes filed a formal complaint about being read aloud for the fourth time, and HR is involved now. (118 chars)
- Original, specific dev artifact (standup notes) + human twist (HR). Good
  escalation. Slightly wordier than #1 and "HR is involved now" is a touch
  abstract; the laugh depends on the HR escalation landing.

### 4. The recording asked me not to come
> I skipped it because the meeting's own recording asked me not to come — it has seen the last three. (98 chars)
- Clever meta twist, concrete (the recording), tight. But "the recording"
  is less universally felt than the printer; the joke is a bit cerebral and
  the turn arrives late.

### 5. Laptop closed itself out of respect
> I tried to join, but my laptop opened last week's notes and closed itself out of respect. (88 chars)
- Nice anthropomorphism, very tight. But the turn is gentle rather than sharp;
  "closed itself out of respect" is charming, not a laugh. Loses to #1 on
  criterion 2.

### 6. Jira ticket moved to backlog
> I can't make it; my Jira ticket for "attend meeting" just got moved to the backlog by someone with better priorities. (117 chars)
- Real dev artifact, relatable. But it explains itself a little too much
  ("by someone with better priorities" spells out the joke) and reads more
  like a status update than a punchline.

### 7. Git blame recusal
> Git blame shows I was the last person to speak in that meeting, so I've recused myself from all future ones. (109 chars)
- Dev artifact + human twist, concrete. But "recused myself" is formal and
  the premise needs a beat to parse — an excuse that needs explaining has
  already lost the laugh.

## Verdict

Candidate 1 wins: the sharpest turn (a 1:1 with the printer), the most
concrete office object, the tightest line, and the freshest angle. It is the
only one where the punchline is also a quiet image — you can picture the
person choosing the printer over the meeting.
